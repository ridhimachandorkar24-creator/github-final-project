# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free environment for everyone who participates in this project, regardless of age, background, experience, identity, or level of technical knowledge.

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior include:

- Being respectful and considerate toward others.
- Welcoming constructive feedback and different perspectives.
- Giving and accepting feedback professionally.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its community.
- Showing empathy and respect toward other contributors.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, insulting or discriminatory comments, and any conduct that creates an intimidating or hostile environment.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards of this Code of Conduct. They may take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including the repository, issue discussions, pull requests, and other project-related communication channels. It also applies when an individual officially represents the project or its community.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers through the repository's available contact or reporting channels.

Reports will be reviewed fairly and, to the extent reasonably possible, handled confidentially. Maintainers will take appropriate action based on the nature and severity of the incident.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Project maintainers may follow these guidelines when responding to violations:

1. **Correction:** A private or public warning requesting that the behavior stop.
2. **Warning:** A formal warning about the behavior and its consequences.
3. **Temporary Suspension:** Temporary restriction from participating in project spaces.
4. **Permanent Removal:** Permanent removal from project spaces for serious or repeated violations.

The response will be proportionate to the severity and circumstances of the violation.
